BOARD FIELD RULES
  Organization: a registered organization slug, e.g. nal, blueprint, wheeling-miners
  Audience: optional additional registered slugs, comma-separated
  Campaign: shared campaign name
  Publish At ET: YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM, e.g. 2026-10-26T12:00 (Eastern)
  Platform: exact platform names, comma-separated when the caption is shared
  Approval: Draft, Pending Review, or Approved (exact capitalization)
  Content Role: Main Post, Share, Story, Reply, or Video
  Asset Link: complete HTTPS image/video/download URL
  Asset Type: Image, Video, or Document
  Original Post Link: actual social post URL for shares/replies

Monday is the master source for content and approval. Refresh pulls live board content when the connection is configured. During connection outages, a dated saved snapshot remains available; connection warnings are shown. Items removed from the board or moved into a group titled Archived are removed from staff views after a successful full sync.

Publication records are stored per item, organization, and platform in the hub. Repeated saves update the same record. Staff manually publish on the social platforms and then record the resulting link. This version does not auto-post, retrieve CPM metrics, or write publication completion back into Monday.
